# Print and assembly sequence

1. Print `18_HeatSet_Insert_Test_Coupon.stl` and select correct pilots for your printer/filament.
2. Print structural parts in PETG/ASA-class material for prototype outdoor use; TPU gasket separately.
3. Heat-install threaded brass inserts while parts are supported and square.
4. Assemble the TAL220B fixed/floating load-cell stack and verify free movement.
5. Bolt center housing to floating platform.
6. Install 6 mm D-shaft through rotor.
7. Install bearing-side plate, 696 bearing and retainer cap.
8. Install servo-side plate and MG996R cradle.
9. Attach purchased 25T aluminum hub to servo, then bolt printed drive adapter to the hub and engage the D-shaft.
10. Fit hopper adapter, TPU gasket, hopper and lid.
11. Fit trough slide rail and slide trough into place.
12. Mount electronics, strain-relief the cables, wire HX711/XIAO/servo power, then calibrate.
13. Dry-test with measured feed before placing near animals.
