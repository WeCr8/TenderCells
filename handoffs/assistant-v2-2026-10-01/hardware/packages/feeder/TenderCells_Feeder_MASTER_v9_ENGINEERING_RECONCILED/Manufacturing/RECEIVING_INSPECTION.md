# Receiving Inspection Before Batch Production

1. Record supplier, exact SKU and lot.
2. Measure critical component dimensions with calipers.
3. Compare to `CAD_MODEL_CLASSIFICATION.csv`.
4. Replace a reference-envelope STEP if the purchased part differs.
5. Print and validate the heat-set insert coupon.
6. Dry-assemble shaft, bearing, rotor and stepper coupler.
7. Verify the load-cell floating platform has no hard bypass to the fixed base.
8. Calibrate with known weights.
9. Run at least 100 dry indexing cycles and then a feed-loaded endurance check before classroom deployment.
10. Verify local stop/interlock behavior before allowing any remote command request.
