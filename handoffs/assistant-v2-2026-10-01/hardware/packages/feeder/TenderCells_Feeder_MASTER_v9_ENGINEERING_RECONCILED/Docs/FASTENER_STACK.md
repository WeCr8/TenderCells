# Fastener / insert stack

- M3 brass heat-set inserts: enclosure lid, hopper keeper, servo bracket interfaces, bearing retainer, shaft adapter set screw.
- M4 brass heat-set inserts: center housing structural joints and feeder mounting.
- M5 brass heat-set inserts: TAL220B load-cell mounting.
- Rotor drive hub: 4 x M3 screws through printed adapter into purchased 20 mm aluminum 25T hub.
- Bearing: 696 / 619/6 family, 6 mm ID x 15 mm OD x 5 mm wide.
- Shaft: 6 mm x ~100 mm steel D-shaft or 6 mm shaft with a machined/filed flat.

Print the included heat-set coupon before the feeder and tune actual hole size to the inserts purchased.
