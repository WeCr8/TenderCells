## One-line description

## Description
TenderCells connects Claude to your own smart animal-care property.

Ask Claude to:
- check farm/device status;
- surface animal-care warnings;
- review predator and yard events;
- explore TenderCells learning missions;
- guide verified hardware Builder projects one step at a time.

The hosted TenderCells connector is read-only. It cannot open doors, dispense feed, switch relays, drive robots, or clear E-STOP. Physical control remains in TenderCells OS or the local farm-network integration with explicit safety confirmation.

TenderCells is also an open-source learning platform for students, makers, farmers and engineers.
