Starter: no ASIN or price is given on purpose. Pick a listing that meets every minimum above and verify its force, stroke, current and hole size before buying; if its speed is below ~8 mm/s, raise `CT_TRAVEL_TIMEOUT_MS` in `firmware/chicken-tender-starter/platformio.ini`.

The ~2.0 A driver limit caps any actuator's current regardless of its rating; a door load of ~15 N is a small fraction of either actuator's rating.
