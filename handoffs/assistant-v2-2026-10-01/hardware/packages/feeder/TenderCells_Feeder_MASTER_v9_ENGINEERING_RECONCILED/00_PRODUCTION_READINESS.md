# Production Readiness Status

## Suitable now
Controlled prototype and classroom pilot, after incoming dimensional verification of the purchased electronics/hardware. The package includes validated printable geometry, neutral STEP solids, BOM/electrical data, firmware, app integration, GLB digital twin, brand assets, dimensions and assembly guidance.

## Still required for formal mass-production release
Supplier/SKU lock, formal revision-controlled manufacturing drawings/GD&T where needed, material-grade specifications, endurance/jam/environmental testing, sanitation validation for the chosen feed-contact strategy, electrical compliance decisions, and final acceptance/inspection criteria. Generic marketplace electronics must not be assumed dimensionally identical.

The v5 STLs were regenerated from valid STEP solids and checked independently for watertightness. See `Validation/STL_VALIDATION_V5.json`.
