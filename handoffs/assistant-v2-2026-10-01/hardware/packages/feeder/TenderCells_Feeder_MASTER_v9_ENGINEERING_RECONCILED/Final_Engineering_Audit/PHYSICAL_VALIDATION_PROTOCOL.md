# Physical Validation Protocol — Required Before Production Release

## Feed capture/dose
Test at minimum: one representative layer pellet and one representative layer crumble. For each, record bulk density using a known-volume cup, then perform 30 individual 60° dispenses. Record grams/index, mean, standard deviation, min/max, incomplete pockets, bridging and fines bypass. Repeat at hopper 100%, 50%, 20% and near-empty levels.

Acceptance target for prototype: no hard jam; no visible continuous bypass stream with rotor stationary; coefficient of variation of dose should be characterized before choosing a production threshold. Do not invent a tolerance until data exists.

## Jam test
Intentionally introduce oversized/awkward particles representative of field contamination. Confirm motor/driver/electronics fail safely without overheating. Measure current and use that data to lock the fuse/PTC and determine whether 28BYJ-48 remains appropriate.

## Load-cell test
Calibrate using at least five known weights spanning the operating range. Check zero return, hysteresis, repeatability and whether touching wires/guards/trough changes the reading. Apply safe external push tests to confirm overload guard prevents destructive loading.

## Environmental/cleaning
Evaluate dust ingress, feed fines accumulation, condensation/water pathways, UV exposure if outdoors, and disassembly for cleaning. Printed feed-contact surfaces require a deliberate material/cleaning decision before field production.
