# Vendor/open CAD sources

- Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32C3: Seeed publishes DXF dimensions and links a 3D model from its official wiki. The official DXF archive is included when available.
- Soldered Electronics HX711: open-source hardware under TAPR OHL; their repository provides a production 3D STEP model. Included in `Electronics/Vendor_CAD` with source/license notes.
- TAL220B 5 kg load cell: modeled from SparkFun/TAL220B published dimensional drawing (55 x 12.7 x 12.7 mm, 2-M5 on 40 mm centers).
- MG996R: reference geometry based on TowerPro published dimensions; clone servos vary, so verify your exact purchased servo before final production.
- 696 bearing: 6 x 15 x 5 mm reference based on NTN dimensional listing.
