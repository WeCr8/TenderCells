# Tender Cells Smart Feeder v3 Mechanical Architecture

## Rotor drive
MG996R positional servo -> purchased 25T aluminum hub -> printed 4-bolt drive adapter -> 6 mm D-shaft -> D-bore rotor -> 696 bearing (6x15x5) -> bearing retainer cap.

The aluminum servo hub isolates the printed part from the servo spline. The 20 mm hub reference uses four M3 holes on a 14 mm bolt circle. The 6 mm shaft has a flat; both rotor and adapter use D geometry to transmit torque positively.

## Serviceability
The cylindrical metering chamber is closed with two removable side plates. Either side can be removed to service the rotor/shaft. The bearing is retained from the outside by a three-screw cap. The trough uses a removable slide rail and should be removed for washing.

## Feed path
Food-contact pieces: hopper, hopper adapter, rotor, center metering housing, trough. Electronics and load cell stay outside the feed path. Use a washable filament/material appropriate for the classroom use case and do not assume ordinary FDM surfaces are sanitary for long-term food contact.
