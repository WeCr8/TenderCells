# Chicken Tender - family build guide (coop + run)

**Document ID:** TC-CHKN-BUILD-001 | **Revision:** A | **Date:** 2026-11-20 | **Status:** Draft, not yet built

A real backyard coop you can build with a 10-year-old helper in two or three weekends, from
plain lumber-yard parts: 2x4, 2x2, plywood, screws, hinges and **1/2 in hardware cloth**.
It is built so the Chicken Tender electronics (automatic door, temperature and ammonia
sensors, camera, feeder) can be added later without tearing anything apart.

> **How to read the steps.** Steps marked **[KID]** are good jobs for the helper (with an adult
> nearby). Steps marked **[ADULT]** use a power saw, a drill overhead or a ladder, or lift heavy
> parts - an adult does those, and the helper can hold the tape, mark lines and hand up screws.
> Everyone wears safety glasses. Gloves for hardware cloth and pressure-treated wood.

## 2. Predator rules (why hardware cloth, not chicken wire)

Chicken wire keeps chickens in; it does **not** keep raccoons, coyotes, dogs, skunks or
rats out (raccoons reach through and tear it). Everything open on this build uses
**1/2 in, 19-gauge galvanized hardware cloth**:

- Every opening bigger than 1/2 in is covered: run walls, run roof, under-coop skirt,
  vents, window.
- Screw it down with **3/4 in screws + 1 in fender washers every 4 in** along every edge (staples pull out).
- **Apron:** a 12 in strip of hardware cloth laid flat on the ground all the way around the
  outside, screwed to the bottom rails and pinned/covered with soil or pavers, so diggers hit
  wire.
- **Two-step latches** on every door (barrel bolt + locking carabiner). Raccoons open single latches.
- The pop door closes by gravity and is checked by the door reed switch (with the electronics).

## 6. Build steps

Plan: **Day 1** coop floor and posts. **Day 2** walls, roof. **Day 3** run frame, doors.
**Day 4** hardware cloth, apron, paint, nest box, roost, ramp. Paint and let it cure several
days before the chickens move in.

### Step 0 - Pick and level the spot
1. **[KID]** Choose a flat, well-drained spot with afternoon shade, at least 3 ft from fences
   (so nothing jumps in), and near enough to an outdoor GFCI outlet for the electronics later.
2. **[KID]** Mark a 4 ft x 12 ft rectangle with string and stakes. Measure both diagonals -
   when they match, it's square.
3. **[ADULT]** Scrape it level. Set a flat paver under each post spot if the ground is soft.

### Step 1 - Cut the parts
1. **[KID]** Measure and mark every cut with a speed square (adult checks each mark).
2. **[ADULT]** Cut all lumber on the saw. **[KID]** Label each part with its ID and stack by ID.
3. **[ADULT]** Cut plywood panels with a circular saw and a straightedge; cut the holes
   (pop door, cleanout door, nest box, window) with a jigsaw - drill a starter hole in each corner.
   Keep the cleanout cut-out: it becomes the door.

### Step 2 - Floor frame
1. **[KID]** Lay C1, C2, C2, C1 on a flat surface as a 48 x 48 box (C2s inside the C1s).
2. **[KID with adult holding]** Drive two 3 in screws through each C1 into the end of each C2.
   Add C3 in the middle the same way.
3. **[KID]** Measure the diagonals; nudge until they match.

### Step 3 - Posts
1. **[KID]** Stand a post in each inside corner of the floor frame: C4 (75-1/2) at the front,
   C5 (63-1/2) at the back. The 3-1/2 in face of each post goes against the front or back rim.
2. **[ADULT]** Lift the frame so its bottom edge is 24 in above the ground (use 24 in blocks),
   check level, then screw each post to both rims with four 3 in screws each way.
3. **[ADULT]** Stand the coop up. Check the posts are plumb with the level.

### Step 4 - Floor
1. **[KID]** Test-fit the floor P1 (corners notched). **[ADULT]** fixes the notches if tight.
2. **[KID]** Screw it to the rims and joist every 8 in with 1-5/8 in screws.

### Step 5 - Walls
1. **[ADULT + KID]** Hold the front wall P2 against the front, bottom edge flush with the bottom
   of the floor frame. **[KID]** drives 1-5/8 in screws every 8 in into the rim and posts.
2. Same for the back wall P3 and both side walls P4 (window on the left, nest box hole on the right).
3. **[KID]** Screw the top nailers C9/C10 inside along the top edge of each wall.
4. There is now a 3-1/2 in gap between the wall tops and the post tops: that is the vent.

### Step 6 - Roof **[ADULT]** (ladder, overhead drilling)
1. Screw headers C6 between the post tops, flush with the tops.
2. Set the three rafters C7 on the headers (left edge, center, right edge), 6 in overhang front
   and back. Toe-screw two 3 in screws each end.
3. Screw the purlins C8 across the rafters: one 2 in from the front end, one in the middle, one
   2 in from the back end.
4. Lay the two corrugated panels (cut to 60 in with tin snips **[ADULT]**), overlapping one rib,
   ribs running front-to-back so rain runs to the back. Roofing screws through the **top** of
   the ribs into each purlin, every other rib. Slope is 3:12, the minimum most panels allow.
5. **[KID]** Cover the front and back vent gaps with hardware cloth strips (gloves!).

### Step 7 - Run frame
1. **[KID]** Lay base rails R1 from the coop's front posts out 8 ft, end rail R2 across the end,
   and R3 around the under-coop area. **[ADULT]** squares and screws them.
2. **[ADULT + KID]** Stand run posts R4 inside the base-rail corners at 4 ft and 8 ft from the
   coop; screw to the base rails.
3. **[ADULT]** (ladder) Screw top rails R5 from the coop's front posts to the end posts at 72 in,
   then cross rails R6 at the middle and end. Add the four corner braces R7.
4. **[KID]** Check every corner with the speed square and the diagonals of the run.

### Step 8 - Doors
1. **Run door [KID + ADULT]:** screw R8/R9 into a 37 x 66 frame (measure the opening between the end posts first; leave 1/4 in each side), add the diagonal brace R10 from
   low on the hinge side to high on the latch side. Cover with hardware cloth. **[ADULT]** hangs
   it on three T-hinges, adds the gate latch + a barrel bolt + carabiner.
2. **Cleanout door [KID]:** screw the C16 2x2 frame to the back of the cut-out panel P3, hinge it
   at the bottom (it folds down like a tailgate) or the side, two barrel bolts + carabiners.
3. **Pop door [KID]:** screw guides C14 + lips C15 on the **inside** of the front wall either side
   of the pop-door hole so the 12 x 13 door P5 slides up and down freely (1/8 in slack).
   Until the electronics go in, a cord over a screw-eye and a cleat lets you open/close it by hand.

### Step 9 - Hardware cloth and apron (gloves on)
1. **[KID]** Unroll cloth along a run side; **[ADULT]** cuts with tin snips (sharp edges - fold
   them under). Lower 48 in band first, then a 24 in band above, overlapping 2 in.
2. **[KID]** Screw it to every rail and post with 3/4 in screws + fender washers every 4 in.
3. Run roof: one 48 in wide piece over the top rails. End wall above the door: a strip.
4. Under-coop skirt: 24 in cloth around the sides and back from the R3 rails up to the coop floor.
5. Apron: 12 in cloth flat on the ground, screwed to the outside of every base rail, pinned with
   landscape staples and covered with soil or pavers.
6. **[KID]** Walk around and look for any gap bigger than a pencil. Fix it.

### Step 10 - Inside the coop
1. **Roost [KID]:** screw roost studs C11 to the inside of each side wall 14 in from the back wall;
   screw cleats C13 at 20 in above the floor; drop the roost bar C12 on top (wide face up), screw
   down. Put the boot tray on the floor under it.
2. **Nest box [ADULT cuts, KID assembles]:** build the 2-hole box from P6 with glue + 1-5/8 in
   screws, slide it over the right-side hole so its floor is 4 in above the coop floor, screw
   through the wall into the box. Hinge the lid (slope it so rain runs off; 1 in overhang).
3. **Ramp [KID]:** screw 1x2 cleats every 6 in on P7; hook it under the pop door sill.
4. **[KID]** 3-4 in of pine shavings on the floor and in the nest boxes (deep litter grows to 8-10 in over time).

### Step 11 - Paint and finish
1. **[KID]** Paint the outside of the coop and the door frames (not inside surfaces the birds peck).
2. **[ADULT]** Add corner trim C17. Let paint cure several days before birds move in.

### Final safety check (do it together)
- [ ] No gaps > 1/2 in anywhere; apron all the way around.
- [ ] Every door has two latches; the carabiners are clipped.
- [ ] No sharp screw tips or wire ends inside; fold or file them.
- [ ] Roost is higher than the nest boxes.
- [ ] Vents open, window covered, roof screws sealed.

## 7. Electronics mounting and wire runs

Pin-by-pin wiring lives in `docs/CHICKEN_TENDER_PINOUT.md` (added with the firmware fixes in the
PR stacked on #180). Boards: **Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3** door controller
(`firmware/chicken-tender-starter`), **ESP32-WROOM-32 DevKit V1** coop sensor/feeder controller
(`firmware/chicken-tender`, Pro add-on), **XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense** camera (`firmware/camera-node`).

| Part | Where it mounts | Height / position | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Control box** (XIAO door controller + DRV8874 + 5 V regulator; later the WROOM board in the same or a second box) | **Outside the back wall**, upper right, under the 6 in roof overhang | top of box at ~56 in above ground (back wall top is 60 in) | out of the ammonia and dust inside, dry under the eave, an adult can reach it, kids can see the lights |
| **NC E-STOP** (22 mm mushroom, NC contacts only: 2 blocks Base/Starter, 3 Plus/Pro) | on the control box lid (under-coop bay builds: a 1-hole station on the back wall, 43 in) | same | push to stop the door motor in hardware |
| **12 V Class 2 adapter** | outdoor GFCI outlet with an in-use cover | - | only low voltage reaches the coop |
| **Door actuator** (12 V, 12 in stroke) | **inside, along the top of the front wall**, lying horizontally on a 2x4 mounting board screwed to the front posts, clevis pins at both ends | ~3 in below the front nailer C9 | cord from the rod end goes through a cord eye centered over the pop door, down to the door anchor; retracting lifts the door 11 in; the door falls closed by its own weight |
| **Door reeds** (closed + open) | magnet on the pop door, one reed on the guide at the closed position, one at the open (11 in up) position | at the door | confirms the door really closed |
| **Temp/humidity** (one DHT22, single-wire: XIAO D4 on Base/Starter, WROOM GPIO4 on Plus/Pro; the I2C DHT20 is no longer used) | inside, left side wall, mid-depth | 14 in above the floor (chicken height), away from the window draft | measures what the birds feel |
| **MQ-137 ammonia** (Pro) | inside, left side wall, behind a small hardware-cloth cage | 12 in above the floor, at least 18 in from the roost (droppings) | ammonia builds up low, near the litter |
| **Camera** (XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense, small box) | inside, front-left upper corner, aimed at the roost and pop door | ~6 in below the roof | headcount at night; a second camera outside the back wall can watch the run |
| **Feeder** (gravity now, motor + load cell later) | in the run, under the coop, hung from the floor joist C3 | bottom at chicken back height (~8-10 in) | shaded, dry, out of the rain |

### Wire routes
- **Through the back wall:** drill 3 holes **[ADULT]** just below the control box, each with a
  PG7 cable gland: (1) actuator cable, (2) sensor cables, (3) camera/spare power.
- **Inside the coop:** run cables up the back-left post, then along the **underside of the left
  side top nailer C10** to the front wall, held with cable clips every 12 in, above the birds'
  reach. Drop to each sensor down the side wall inside a 1/2 in PVC conduit or a 1x2 cover strip.
- **Actuator and reeds:** along the inside of the front nailer C9 to the actuator and down the
  door guides to the reeds.
- **Feeder (later):** from the control box down the back post, under the floor along the joist C3,
  to the feeder. Drip loop at every gland.
- Approximate cable lengths: actuator 1.5 m, each reed 1.2 m, each sensor 1.2 m, camera 1.5 m,
  feeder 1.5 m (matches the Starter wiring harness W13-W20).

## 8. Later smart features (in order)

1. **Automatic door** - opens at sunrise, closes after dusk, with a light-sensor or clock rule;
   the closed reed proves it shut; the app alerts if it didn't. (Starter firmware + app routines.)
2. **Temperature and humidity** - alert below 35 F or above 85 F (the app's existing thresholds).
3. **Ammonia** - MQ-137 in ppm after a clean-air calibration; 10 ppm = add bedding / ventilate,
   25 ppm = clean now. Reads `null` until calibrated so it never false-alarms.
4. **Camera** - night headcount on the roost and a look at the run from the phone.
5. **Feeder** - motor-dispensed portions (500 g cap per command) and a load cell for "feed low".
6. Later (Pro): poop-tray cassette, water level, predator alerts (WatchTower), rail robot.
