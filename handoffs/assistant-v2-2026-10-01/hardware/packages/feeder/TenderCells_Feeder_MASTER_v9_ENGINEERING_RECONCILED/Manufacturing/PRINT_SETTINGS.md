# Recommended Prototype Print Settings

- Structural parts: PETG, 0.20 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 5 top/bottom layers, 25–35% infill.
- Rotor: PETG, 0.16–0.20 mm layers, 4–5 perimeters; inspect shaft bore and pocket edges.
- Load-cell base/platform: PETG, 5 perimeters, 35–45% infill; keep mounting faces flat.
- Heat-set coupon: print first with the exact machine/material profile used for production.
- Hopper gasket: TPU, 1.8 mm modeled thickness; tune for selected material.
- Do not scale STLs. Reparameterize the CAD.
- For long-term live-feed use, keep feed-contact components removable and cleanable and use an appropriate smooth liner/container where required.
