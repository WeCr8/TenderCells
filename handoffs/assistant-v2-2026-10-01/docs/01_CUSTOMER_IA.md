# Customer information architecture

## Farm Home
Farm status, needs attention, recent activity, systems online, and quick links.

## Alerts
Health, predator, fault, connectivity and unresolved yard events.

## Animals
Use **Animals** in customer UI rather than Pets. Legacy internal naming may remain during migration.

## Habitats
Chicken Tender, Duck Dock and future habitat twins.

## Property Twin
Property → environment → animals → habitats → devices → robots, with provenance where useful.

## Builder
Project library, current build, one-step instruction pages, parts, checkpoints and existing `/flash` handoff.

## Missions
Explore the Farm; Protect the Chickens; Beat the Heat; Robot Traffic Jam; Build a Coop Brain; Sensor Detective.

## Devices
Connected hardware, online state, latest telemetry and firmware identity where available.
