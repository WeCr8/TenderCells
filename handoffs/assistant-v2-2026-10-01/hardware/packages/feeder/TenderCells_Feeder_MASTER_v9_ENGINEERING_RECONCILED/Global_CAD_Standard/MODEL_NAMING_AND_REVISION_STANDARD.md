# Model Naming and Revision Standard

Format: `TC-<FAMILY>-<TYPE>-<NUMBER>_<DESCRIPTION>_R<REV>.<ext>` for released future assets.

Family examples: `FDR` feeder, `CT` Chicken Tender, `DD` Duck Dock, `RR` Roaming Roost, `PM` Predator Monitor, `GLOBAL` shared.
Type examples: `PRT`, `ASM`, `SUB`, `PCB`, `REF`, `GLB`, `DWG`.

Never encode software-package revision (v3/v4/v5) into the engineering part description. Product revision and package release are separate metadata fields. v6 retains some legacy filenames only under `LEGACY_REFERENCE`.
