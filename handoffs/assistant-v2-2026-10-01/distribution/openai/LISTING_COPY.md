## Short description
Connect TenderCells to check your own farm in plain language: device status, coop readings, health warnings, yard events, predator alerts, Builder projects, and learning missions. The hosted connector is read-only and never moves hardware.

## Longer description
TenderCells is an open-source animal-care automation platform for smart coops, sensors, cameras, robots, and hands-on learning.

Use the plugin to:
- check the status of your TenderCells farm;
- review animal-care warnings and device health;
- inspect recent predator and yard events;
- explore TenderCells learning missions;
- follow verified Builder projects one step at a time.

The hosted connector is read-only by design. Physical hardware actions stay in TenderCells OS or the local farm-network integration, where safety checks and explicit confirmation remain in control.

No hardware yet? TenderCells also maintains a simulated demo environment for learning and review.
